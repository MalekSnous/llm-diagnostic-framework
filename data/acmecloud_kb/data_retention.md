# Data Retention and Recovery

## Log retention

Pipeline logs are retained for 30 days on Starter, 180 days on Growth, and
400 days on Enterprise. On the Growth plan and above, logs can be exported to
external storage before they expire.

## Deletion and recovery

Deleted datasets are recoverable from the trash for 21 days before permanent
deletion. When an account is closed, all remaining customer data is purged
60 days after closure.

## Backups

Automated backups run daily and are retained for 35 days. Point-in-time
recovery is available on the Enterprise plan only, with a 7-day recovery
window.

## Dataset versioning

Dataset version history is kept for 90 days on the Growth plan and indefinitely
on the Enterprise plan. The Starter plan does not include dataset versioning.
