# Plans and Billing

## Plans and limits

AcmeCloud offers three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise.

The Starter plan allows up to 5 concurrent pipelines and 50 GB of managed storage.
The Growth plan allows up to 50 concurrent pipelines and 2 TB of managed storage.
The Enterprise plan allows unlimited concurrent pipelines and 40 TB of managed
storage.

The Starter plan costs $49 per month. The Growth plan costs $499 per month.
Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated per contract. Annual billing gives a
15% discount on any plan. New customers can take a 30-day free trial that
unlocks all Growth-plan features.

## API rate limits

Each plan includes a soft API rate limit: 600 requests per minute on Starter,
3,000 requests per minute on Growth, and 20,000 requests per minute on
Enterprise.

## Compute billing (ACU)

Billing is computed per "ACU" (Acme Compute Unit). One ACU equals 4 vCPUs and
16 GB of RAM running for one hour.

The Starter plan includes 100 free ACUs per month, with additional ACUs billed
at $0.12 each. The Growth plan includes 500 free ACUs per month, with additional
ACUs billed at $0.08 each. The Enterprise plan includes 5,000 free ACUs per
month, with additional ACUs billed at $0.05 each.

## Invoicing and payment

Invoices are issued on the 3rd of each month. Accepted payment methods are
credit card on all plans; payment by wire transfer is available on the
Enterprise plan only. Plan upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take
effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
