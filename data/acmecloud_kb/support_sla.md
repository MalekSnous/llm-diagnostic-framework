# Support and SLAs

## Support response times

Starter plan support response time is 48 business hours. Growth plan support
response time is 8 business hours, and severity-1 incidents on the Growth plan
are answered within 4 hours. Enterprise plan severity-1 incidents are answered
within 1 hour, 24/7.

The escalation contact for severity-1 Enterprise incidents is the on-call
address sev1@acmecloud.example.

## Support channels

Email support is included in every plan. Live chat support is available on the
Growth plan and above. Enterprise customers get a dedicated Technical Account
Manager (TAM).

## Uptime SLA

The monthly uptime SLA is 99.5% on Starter, 99.9% on Growth, and 99.95% on
Enterprise. When the SLA is missed, customers receive a service credit of 10%
of the monthly fee per 0.1% of availability below the target, capped at 30% of
the monthly fee.

## Maintenance

Scheduled maintenance happens on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC in each
region, announced at least 72 hours in advance on status.acmecloud.example.
