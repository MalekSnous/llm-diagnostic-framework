AcmeCloud Platform — Internal Product Reference (v3.2)

# Overview
AcmeCloud is Acme Corp's internal data-orchestration platform. This document is
the source of truth for support agents. The facts below are specific to AcmeCloud
and are NOT public knowledge — a general-purpose LLM cannot know them without
retrieval.

# Plans and Limits
The Starter plan allows up to 5 concurrent pipelines and 50 GB of managed storage.
The Growth plan allows up to 50 concurrent pipelines and 2 TB of managed storage.
The Enterprise plan allows unlimited concurrent pipelines and 40 TB of managed storage.
Each plan includes a soft API rate limit of 600 requests per minute on Starter,
3,000 requests per minute on Growth, and 20,000 requests per minute on Enterprise.

# Authentication
AcmeCloud uses a token called an "AKT" (Acme Key Token). AKTs expire after 14 days
by default. An AKT can be rotated without downtime using the `acme auth rotate`
command. Service accounts use a longer-lived token called an "AKT-S" which expires
after 90 days.

# Regions
AcmeCloud is available in three regions: eu-paris-1, us-reston-2, and ap-osaka-3.
The default region for new accounts created after January 2025 is eu-paris-1.
Cross-region replication is only supported between eu-paris-1 and us-reston-2.

# Billing
Billing is computed per "ACU" (Acme Compute Unit). One ACU equals 4 vCPUs and
16 GB of RAM running for one hour. The Growth plan includes 500 free ACUs per month;
additional ACUs are billed at $0.08 each. Invoices are issued on the 3rd of each month.

# Support SLAs
Starter plan support response time is 48 business hours. Growth plan is 8 business
hours. Enterprise plan is 1 hour for severity-1 incidents, 24/7. The escalation
contact for severity-1 Enterprise incidents is the on-call address sev1@acmecloud.example.

# Data Retention
Pipeline logs are retained for 30 days on Starter, 180 days on Growth, and 400 days
on Enterprise. Deleted datasets are recoverable from the trash for 21 days before
permanent deletion.

# Known Limitations
The AcmeCloud Python SDK (package name `acmecloud-sdk`) requires Python 3.10 or newer.
The legacy `acme-py` package is deprecated and will be removed in v4.0, scheduled for
March 2026. Webhooks currently support a maximum payload of 256 KB.
